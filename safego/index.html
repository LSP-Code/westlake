About alerts in this app

SafeGo runs as a web app, so alerts work like this:

While this tab is open — everyone in the circle gets an instant full-screen alert, siren, vibration, and spoken announcement, and can check in.

Custom alarm sound — while SafeGo is open (even in the background tab or as an installed home-screen app), the panic alert plays the real SafeGo alarm MP3, on loop, at full volume.

What it can't do — no browser on iPhone, Android, or Chrome lets a website set a custom sound for its system notification banner; the phone always uses its own default ding for that banner. It also cannot ring through Silent Mode as an Apple Critical Alert, or draw over other apps the way an installed native app can. Those both require a native app built and approved for each platform. Keep this tab open (or the installed app running) and your volume up during drills and real events.
